SLIDE 2: THE PROBLEM WITH ONCHAIN GAMING & CASINOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Modern Web3 gaming and prediction arenas suffer from critical structural flaws:

1. Predatory House Edges & Rigged Odds:
   - Traditional crypto casinos take 2% to 15% house cuts and tilt odds against
     players. Casual gamers want fair 50/50 peer-to-peer competition.

2. Mempool Sniping & Front-Running:
   - In naive onchain turn-based games, players who submit moves publicly can be
     front-run by miners, MEV bots, or opponents monitoring the mempool.

3. The "Losing Player Freeze" / Griefing Dilemma:
   - In two-player commit games, if a player reveals their winning move first, the
     losing player can simply walk away or disconnect—freezing funds indefinitely
     or forcing awkward arbitration.

4. Centralized Oracle & VRF Vulnerabilities:
   - Reliance on off-chain random number generators (RNG) or oracle relays introduces
     latency, exorbitant subscription gas overhead, and single points of failure.

5. Reentrancy & Unsafe Push-Payment Exploits:
   - Insecure payout mechanisms that push funds directly to external contracts can
     be blocked by malicious receiver fallbacks, freezing the whole platform.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SLIDE 3: THE SOLUTION - POTSHOT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PotShot solves every one of these problems with pure cryptographic game theory:

- 100% Peer-to-Peer with Zero House Edge:
  - 100% of the pot goes to the winner (2x stake). Draws refund both players in full.
  - No platform fees, no protocol take rates, no hidden taxes.

- Cryptographic Commit-Reveal:
  - Moves remain completely confidential during commitment:
    commitment = keccak256(abi.encode(player, move, salt))
  - Neither the opponent nor block builders can inspect choices before locking in.

- Address-Bound Anti-Copy Protection:
  - Binding the caller's address directly into the hash preimage guarantees that an
    opponent cannot blindly copy the creator's commitment hash.

- Automatic Timeout Forfeiture Engine:
  - If one player reveals and the opponent stalls or abandons, a public timeout
    claim automatically awards 100% of the pot to the honest revealer. Hiding a
    losing move provides zero economic advantage.

- Bulletproof Pull-Payment Architecture (`pendingBalance`):
  - Every settlement credits internal balances. Funds leave only when users
    explicitly call withdraw(), guaranteeing that rejecting contracts cannot disrupt
    other players or lock contract state.

SLIDE 4: HOW IT WORKS (THE GAME LOOP)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Step 1: Create Game
- Player A selects Rock, Paper, or Scissors and stakes native BOT.
- Frontend generates a cryptographically secure 32-byte salt and computes:
  commitment = keccak256(abi.encode(playerA, moveA, saltA))
- `createGame(commitment)` locks the stake in `Status.Open`.

Step 2: Join Game
- Player B browses open lobbies and matches the exact stake with their secret move.
- `joinGame(id, commitment)` locks in Player B, records `joinedAt = block.timestamp`,
  and activates the countdown: `revealDeadline = now + revealWindow`.
- Status transitions to `Status.Committed`.

Step 3: Reveal Phase
- Both players reveal their move and salt via `reveal(id, move, salt)`.
- The contract computes `computeCommitment(msg.sender, move, salt)` and validates
  it against the stored commitment.

Step 4: Immediate Settlement & Showdown
- Draw: If moveA == moveB, each player receives their original stake back.
- Decisive Win: Rock beats Scissors, Scissors beats Paper, Paper beats Rock.
  Winner receives 2x stake (entire pot) and win/loss records are updated.
- Timeout / Forfeit: If the deadline expires and only one player revealed, that
  player wins the entire pot. If neither revealed, both are refunded.

SLIDE 5: SMART CONTRACT ARCHITECTURE & SECURITY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Contract: `PotShot.sol` (Solidity ^0.8.24)
- Library: OpenZeppelin Contracts v5.0.2 (`ReentrancyGuard`)

Key Architectural Safeguards:
1. ReentrancyGuard:
   - Applied to all balance-mutating functions (`createGame`, `joinGame`, `cancelGame`,
     `reveal`, `claimTimeout`, `withdraw`).
2. Strict Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI):
   - State variables and `pendingBalance` are updated before events or external transfers.
3. No Direct Value Reception:
   - Zero `receive()` or `fallback()` handlers. Plain transfers automatically revert.
4. Custom Errors:
   - Gas-optimized custom errors (`ZeroStake`, `InvalidReveal`, `OnlyPlayerA`, etc.)
     replace heavy require strings.
5. Immutability & Decentralization:
   - Immutable `revealWindow` set at deployment (1 hour default, 1 min to 7 days bounds).
   - No owner, no admin keys, no pause functions, no upgrade proxies.
